# Commercial license — how to buy / inquire

**Most people never need this.** Building apps and client work with Powers is free under **BSL-1.1** (source-available). See [LICENSING.md](./LICENSING.md).

You need a **commercial license** when you want rights or assurances beyond the free Additional Use Grant — especially a **Competing Offering** waiver, invoice, or priority support.

---

## How to inquire (live now)

1. Open **[lab206.com/contact](https://lab206.com/contact?subject=Commercial%20license)**  
2. Choose subject **Commercial license**  
3. Include: company name, approx team size, use case (app vs kit), tier interest  

Or email **[scott@lab206.com](mailto:scott@lab206.com?subject=Powers%20commercial%20license)** with subject **Powers commercial license**.

You will get a short reply with next steps (quote → invoice → signed confirmation). Self-serve checkout (LemonSqueezy / Gumroad) can plug in later without changing this flow.

## What *not* to pay for

- Installing `@lab206/*` from npm  
- Building dashboards, portals, or client apps **with** Powers  
- Using the free Figma library + Community plugin  

If that’s you: `pnpm create powers my-app` and you’re done.
